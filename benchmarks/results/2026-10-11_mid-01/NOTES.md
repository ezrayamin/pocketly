- `bm-02-run.sh` 5 2 → 1 warm-up + 10 measured iterations per build, alternating blocks of 2.
  Block start temperatures 32.2–36.2 °C (`bm-02/temperatures.csv`); the 40 °C gate never waited.
- **Sample sizes: n = 10 per build for every BM-02 metric**: Flashlight's fps, CPU and RAM, and
  the `dumpsys gfxinfo` metrics (janky frames, frame time p90/p99, frames rendered). Every
  measured iteration has a gfxinfo file.
- **Two iterations lost frame-trace events** (Flashlight logged `Could not parse ATrace line
  "… [LOST n EVENTS]"`): jsCore block 2 #2 (788 events) and jsCore block 4 #1 (79 events).
  Both completed with status `SUCCESS`, so they are kept: the rule (§4.3) is to keep every
  iteration that completed and exclude only failed ones. Lost events affect that iteration's
  trace-based frame rate, not gfxinfo, which counts frames separately. For the record, their
  fps (42.4, 42.2) is inside the other iterations' range, but that is not why they are kept.
- **Frame rates are lower than on 2026-10-05**: median 42.5 fps for both builds here vs
  44.5 (jsCore) / 44.6 (kmpCore) then. Expected between sessions: OS update, temperatures and
  phone state differ. Compare builds within a session, never fps across sessions.
- Janky frames (0.8% vs 0.9% median) have CV > 10%. Under §4.3's rule for rates under 5%, they
  are judged in percentage points: shift +0.1 pp (95% CI −0.3 to +0.5).
- **RenderThread CPU, kmpCore: CV 11.7%, over the 10% rule; kept with this note, not rerun**
  (decision 2026-10-11). Its three highest iterations (40.9, 42.5, 43.4%) are in blocks 4–5,
  while jsCore stayed at 30.7–36.4% throughout. Not heat: kmpCore's block 5 started cooler
  (35.7 °C) than jsCore's (36.0 °C). Shift +0.8 pp (95% CI −1.3 to +6.2), not meaningful. On
  2026-10-05 the same metric had CV 17–20% in both builds.

  js, kmp) with `pocketly.bench`, 5 warm-up + 10 measured runs in-process → 50 per build.
  Battery 29.2–31.8 °C at every launch (`temperatures.csv` in each folder).
- Checksums in `summary.md`: both builds read the same input and produced the same output in
  every scenario, timing and memory rounds alike.
- **Lost logs, relaunched cold starts.** Some cold starts ran (the app showed its result) but
  none of the process's log lines reached logcat, not even ART's or React Native's startup lines;
  a normal start logs ~150 lines within 2 s. The runner now relaunches such a cold start after
  15 s, at most twice, and records it in `retries.csv`; the relaunch is the measured cold start.
  Under §4.3 the lost ones are failed iterations, excluded and counted:

  | Folder | Relaunches | Builds |
  |---|---:|---|

  | `bm-04` | 1 | jsCore |
  | `bm-06` | 0 | |
  | `bm-04/memory` | 5 | 2 jsCore, 3 kmpCore |

  Both builds are affected, so it is the phone, not a build. Possible cause, not confirmed: this
  ROM has a log rate limiter (`log.ratelimit.level=1`; logcat showed `ratelimit: Single process

- **BM-03 was run three times; the third is the result.**

## BM-04 peak memory (memory rounds)
- `MEMORY=1 bm-harness.sh <dir> BM-04`, after the timing rounds: 5 rounds, same protocol, results
  in `bm-04/memory/` (benchmarking.md §5.2). RSS sampled every 3.4 ms (median; max gap 11.1 ms).
  Timings from these rounds aren't used.
- **The clear result is the baseline**: kmpCore's process is ~61 MB bigger before ingesting
  anything (RSS 262.8 vs 201.4 MB, PSS 169.3 vs 108.0 MB, VmHWM 268.9 vs 203.9 MB; CV ≤ 0.6%).
- **The ingest itself adds little in either build**: peak over baseline 3.6 MB (jsCore) vs
  2.3 MB (kmpCore), not meaningful; per call, 0.0 vs 1.8 MB.
- **Swap caveat for kmpCore's ingest numbers.** The phone swaps to zram, and a new process
  inherits swapped-out pages from the zygote. VmSwap stays at 30.7 MB in every jsCore round but
  falls 31.2 → 29.0 MB in every kmpCore round, mostly inside the idle second used as baseline:
  ~2.2 MB of inherited pages paged back in, which RSS counts without any new allocation. That is
  about the size of kmpCore's whole peak-over-baseline, so this session can't tell how much of
  it the ingest allocated. The ~61 MB baseline difference is far above it.
- CV flags on the memory rows (peak over baseline, per-call delta, Java heap PSS) come from
  medians at or near 0 MB and from kmpCore's Java heap being either ~8 or ~26 MB depending on
  when the last GC ran. No rule exempts them; they are reported as is.
